01 / Overview

Digital-service content that reads fast.

The Expert Sol series is built for a busy feed. The visual hierarchy leads with a single service or business message, supports it with a strong central graphic and keeps the orange/black identity present across the set.

Goal

Explain services quickly

Branding, websites, SEO, social and related digital-service messaging.

System

Orange-led identity

Consistent accent color and typography connect the series.

Scope

9 creatives
